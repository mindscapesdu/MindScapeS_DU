Reading List & Handouts

To enrich the question-and-answer period and facilitate deep interdisciplinary engagement, speakers provide preparatory papers and handouts for participants prior to each session.

Active Preparation

Session 2: Prof. Susanna Schellenberg (Nov 6, 2026)

Schellenberg, Susanna. “The Flexibility and Stability of Perspectives.” Proceedings of the Aristotelian Society.

Develops an account of active versus dormant perspectives informed by cognitive psychology, neuroscience, and philosophy of mind. Discusses how perspectives shift through self-concept anchors within broader cognitive schemas.

Archived Readings

Session 1: Prof. Melissa M. Kibbe (Oct 30, 2026)

Kibbe, Melissa M. “Minimal Object Representations in the Infant Mind.” Handout for the Language of Thought Series, Department of Philosophy, University of Delhi.

Session handout introducing object-file tracking, feature binding constraints, and developmental evidence in early infant working memory.
