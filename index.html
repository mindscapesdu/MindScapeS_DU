Speaker & Commentator

Colloquium participants for Session 1:

Featured Speaker

Prof. Melissa Kibbe

Department of Psychological and Brain Sciences
Boston University

Director of the Developing Minds Lab at Boston University. Her research investigates the core architecture of human cognitive development, focusing on how infants and young children represent objects, track spatiotemporal identity, encode numerical values, and construct conceptual knowledge about the physical and social world.

Commentator 1

Prof. Sami Yousif

Department of Psychology (Cognitive Psychology)
The Ohio State University

Assistant Professor of Cognitive Psychology at The Ohio State University. His research explores human perception, space, geometry, and spatial representations, investigating the foundational visual and cognitive formats that bridge low-level perception and abstract higher-level thought.
