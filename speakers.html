Colloquium Directory

Invited Speakers & Commentators

MindScapeS hosts distinguished international researchers whose work bridges epistemology, philosophy of mind, developmental cognition, and perceptual neuroscience.

Keynote Speakers

Speaker • Session 2 (Nov 6)

Prof. Susanna Schellenberg

Rutgers University, USA
Department of Philosophy & Center for Cognitive Science

Susanna Schellenberg is a leading philosopher of mind and cognitive science whose research investigates perception, mental representation, evidence, and the neural substrates of perspectival cognition.

Talk: The Flexibility and Stability of Perspectives
Speaker • Session 1 (Oct 30)

Prof. Melissa M. Kibbe

Boston University, USA
Department of Psychological & Brain Sciences

Melissa Kibbe directs the Developing Minds Lab at Boston University. Her laboratory investigates the foundational cognitive representations of objects, memory architecture, and conceptual development across infancy and childhood.

Talk: Minimal object representations in the infant mind

Commentators & Discussants

Commentator • Session 1

Prof. Sami Yousif

The Ohio State University, USA
Department of Psychology • Cognitive Psychology

Sami Yousif explores spatial cognition, perceptual grouping, numerical representation, and how cognitive systems organize complex visual and mental information across development.

Commentary on: Prof. Melissa Kibbe's colloquium on infant object representation.
